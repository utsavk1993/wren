---
title: Camera feed is black or frozen
device_type: camera
symptoms: camera not working, black screen, frozen image, no live view, camera offline
---

A camera showing black or a frozen frame is usually a connection problem rather
than a broken camera.

1. Open the app and tell me whether the camera shows as offline or whether it shows a frozen picture.
2. If it shows a frozen picture, close the app completely and open it again.
3. Unplug the camera's power for ten seconds, then plug it back in.
4. Give it about two minutes to start up and reconnect.
5. Check the live view again.

If it is still black:

6. Check whether the camera has a small light on it and tell me what colour it is.
7. A solid light usually means it is connected. A blinking light usually means it is still trying.

If the picture is dark rather than black, and this only happens at night, the
infrared night vision may be reflecting off a nearby wall or window. Moving the
camera slightly, or closing a blind behind it, usually fixes that.
