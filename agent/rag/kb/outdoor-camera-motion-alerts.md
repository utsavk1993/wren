---
title: Too many motion alerts from an outdoor camera
device_type: camera
symptoms: too many alerts, camera alerts constantly, notifications every few minutes, alerts from cars
---

Outdoor cameras pick up movement they were never meant to watch: passing cars,
swaying branches, shadows moving across a wall as the sun goes round.

1. Open the app and look at a few of the recent alerts.
2. Tell me what is actually moving in them.

If it is traffic or passers-by:

3. In the camera's settings, find Activity Zones.
4. Draw the zone around only the area you care about, such as the driveway or the front step.
5. Movement outside that zone will stop generating alerts.

If it is branches or shadows:

6. Reduce the motion sensitivity by one step and see whether that settles it.

Most cameras can also be set to alert only on people rather than any movement,
which removes almost all of this.
