---
title: Motion sensor triggering false alarms
device_type: motion_sensor
symptoms: false alarm, motion alerts with nobody home, sensor keeps triggering, random alerts at night
---

Motion sensors detect moving heat. Anything in the room that changes
temperature can look like a person: a heating vent, direct sunlight, a ceiling
fan, or a pet.

1. Tell me roughly where in the room the sensor is mounted.
2. Check whether it is pointing at a heating or cooling vent.
3. Check whether direct sunlight falls on it at any time of day.
4. Check whether a fan, heater, or radiator sits within its view.
5. If any of those apply, the sensor needs to be angled away from them.

If nothing is pointing at it and you have a pet:

6. Tell me the weight of your pet.
7. Sensors can be set to ignore animals under about forty pounds, but the sensor has to be mounted at the right height for that to work.
8. Measure how high off the floor the sensor is. It should be around seven and a half feet.

If false alarms continue after that, the sensor may be faulty and needs replacing.
