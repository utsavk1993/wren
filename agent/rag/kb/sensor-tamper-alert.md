---
title: Tamper alert on a sensor
device_type: door_sensor
also_applies_to: window_sensor, motion_sensor, keypad
symptoms: tamper, tamper alert, device tampered, cover removed, sensor tamper warning
---

A tamper alert means a device's cover has been opened or it has been pulled off
its mounting. It is often triggered by a battery change or by something knocking
into it.

1. Tell me which device the panel names.
2. Go to that device and check whether its cover is properly closed and clipped shut.
3. Check whether it is sitting flat against the wall or door frame rather than hanging loose.
4. If the cover was open, close it fully. You should feel it click.
5. Wait about thirty seconds for the alert to clear.

If the alert does not clear:

6. On the panel, look for the option to clear or acknowledge alerts and select it.

If a tamper alert appears with nobody near the device, and the cover is properly
closed, the device may be faulty or its mounting may have come loose.
