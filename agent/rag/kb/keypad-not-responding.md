---
title: Keypad not responding
device_type: keypad
symptoms: keypad dead, buttons not working, no display, keypad unresponsive, cannot arm or disarm
---

A keypad that shows nothing at all has usually lost power. One that lights up
but does not respond has usually lost its connection to the panel.

1. Press any key and tell me whether the display lights up.

If nothing lights up:

2. Take the keypad off its bracket. It usually lifts up and off.
3. Take the batteries out, wait ten seconds, and put them back in.
4. Put it back on the bracket and try again.
5. If it still shows nothing, the batteries need replacing.

If it lights up but will not accept a code:

6. Check the panel and tell me whether it shows the keypad as connected.
7. If the panel does not see it, the keypad needs to be paired again.
8. Hold the pairing button on the back of the keypad for about ten seconds until it flashes.
9. On the panel, go to Settings, then Devices, then Add Device.
