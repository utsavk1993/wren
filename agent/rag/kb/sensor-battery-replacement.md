---
title: Replacing a sensor battery
device_type: door_sensor
also_applies_to: window_sensor, motion_sensor
symptoms: change battery, replace battery, what battery does it take, battery type
---

Most sensors use a CR123A or CR2032 battery. Both are sold in supermarkets and
hardware shops.

1. Take the cover off the sensor. Most slide off, some pop off with a fingernail.
2. Note which way round the battery sits before removing it.
3. Take the old battery out.
4. Put the new one in the same way round.
5. Put the cover back on.
6. Wait about thirty seconds for the sensor to reconnect.
7. Open and close the door, or walk past the sensor, so it sends a signal.
8. Check that the low battery warning has cleared.

Replacing a battery does not need the system disarmed, but the panel may
announce that the sensor has been tampered with while the cover is off. That
clears on its own once the cover is back on.
