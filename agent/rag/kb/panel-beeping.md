---
title: Panel beeping or chiming repeatedly
device_type: control_panel
symptoms: panel beeping, chirping, chiming, wont stop beeping, annoying noise from panel
---

A panel beeps to tell you something rather than to alarm you. The display names
the reason.

1. Look at the panel and read me what the display says.
2. A single chirp every minute or so usually means a low battery somewhere.
3. A chime each time a door opens is the door chime feature, which can be turned off.

To turn off the door chime:

4. On the panel, go to Settings, then Sounds.
5. Turn Door Chime off.

If the beeping is a low battery warning, the device that needs a new battery
will be named on the display.

If the panel says it has lost mains power, check that its power cable is fully
in the socket and that the socket is not switched off at the wall.
