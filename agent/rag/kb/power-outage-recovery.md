---
title: System behaviour after a power cut
device_type: control_panel
symptoms: power cut, power outage, system after blackout, panel on battery, lost power
---

The panel has a backup battery that keeps the system running for several hours
without mains power.

1. Check whether the panel display is on.
2. If it is on and reporting that it is on battery, the system is still protecting the house.

When mains power comes back:

3. The panel should return to normal on its own within a few minutes.
4. The backup battery then takes up to twenty four hours to recharge fully.

If the panel does not come back after power is restored:

5. Check that its power cable is fully pushed into the socket.
6. Check whether that socket is switched off at the wall, or controlled by a light switch.
7. If the socket is live and the panel is still dead, the power supply may have failed.

Sensors run on their own batteries and are unaffected by a power cut. Cameras
and anything relying on the internet will be offline until the router has power
again.
