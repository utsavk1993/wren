---
title: Low battery warning on a device
device_type: door_sensor
also_applies_to: window_sensor, motion_sensor, keypad
symptoms: low battery, battery warning, panel beeping, device battery low, replace battery
---

Sensors and keypads run on small batteries that last two to three years. A low
battery warning means the device is still working but will stop soon.

1. Tell me which device the warning names.
2. Take the cover off that device. It usually slides or pops off by hand.
3. Look at the battery and tell me what is printed on it. Most are CR123A or CR2032.
4. Take the old battery out.
5. Put the new battery in the same way round as the old one.
6. Put the cover back on.
7. Wait about thirty seconds and check that the warning has cleared from the panel.

The warning can take a few minutes to clear on its own. If it is still showing
after five minutes, open and close the door or walk past the sensor so it sends
a fresh signal.
