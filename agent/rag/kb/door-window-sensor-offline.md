---
title: Door or window sensor showing offline
device_type: door_sensor
also_applies_to: window_sensor
symptoms: sensor offline, sensor not reporting, door shows open when closed, panel says sensor missing
---

A contact sensor stops reporting when its battery dies, when the two halves
have drifted apart, or when it has lost its pairing with the panel.

1. Look at the sensor and tell me whether there is a small light blinking on it.
2. Take the cover off the sensor. It usually slides or pops off by hand.
3. Take the battery out, wait about ten seconds, then put it back in.
4. Put the cover back on and wait about thirty seconds for it to reconnect.
5. Open and close the door once so the sensor sends a fresh signal.
6. Check the panel and tell me whether the sensor still shows offline.

If it is still offline, check the alignment.

7. Look at the two pieces of the sensor. One is on the door, the other on the frame.
8. When the door is closed, they should sit no more than about half an inch apart.
9. If the gap looks wider than that, the sensor cannot see the magnet and will report as open or offline.
