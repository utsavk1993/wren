There are two ways to arm the system.

Arming when leaving the house:

1. Close all doors and windows.
2. On the panel, press Arm Away.
3. Leave through the door you normally use. You have about sixty seconds.

Arming while staying at home:

1. On the panel, press Arm Stay. This arms the doors and windows but leaves the
   motion sensors off so you can move around.

Disarming:

1. Enter your four digit code on the panel or keypad.

If the system refuses to arm, something is open. The panel will name it.

2. Check the door or window the panel names and close it properly.
3. If it is already closed, the sensor on it may be misaligned or offline.
