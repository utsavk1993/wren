---
title: Smoke detector chirping
device_type: control_panel
symptoms: smoke alarm chirping, smoke detector beeping, fire alarm chirp, detector wont stop
---

A chirp every thirty to sixty seconds from a smoke detector almost always means
its battery is low. This is separate from the alarm system's own beeping.

1. Confirm the sound is coming from a smoke detector on the ceiling rather than the alarm panel.
2. Twist the detector anticlockwise to release it from its base.
3. Replace the battery. Most take a nine volt or a CR123A.
4. Twist it back into the base until it locks.
5. Press and hold the test button until it sounds, then release.

If a detector chirps with a fresh battery, it has reached the end of its life.
Detectors expire around ten years after manufacture, and the date is printed on
the back.

If a detector is sounding continuously rather than chirping, treat it as a real
alarm. Get everyone out of the house first and call the fire service.
