---
title: Phone app cannot connect to the system
device_type: control_panel
symptoms: app not working, cannot log in, app says offline, cannot arm from phone, app wont connect
---

The app talks to the panel over the internet, so either end can be the problem.

1. Check whether the panel itself is working. Walk to it and tell me whether the display is on and shows the system as ready.
2. If the panel is offline, the app cannot reach it and the panel needs fixing first.

If the panel looks fine:

3. Close the app completely and open it again.
4. Try turning wifi off on your phone so it uses mobile data instead.
5. If it works on mobile data but not on wifi, the problem is your home network rather than the system.

If it still cannot connect:

6. Sign out of the app and sign back in.
7. Check that the app is up to date.
