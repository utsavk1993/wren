---
title: Glass break sensor triggering or offline
device_type: motion_sensor
symptoms: glass break, glassbreak sensor, sensor triggered by noise, false trigger from sound
---

Glass break sensors listen for the specific sound of breaking glass. Sharp
household noises can occasionally match closely enough to trigger one.

1. Tell me whether the sensor triggered or whether it is showing as offline.

If it triggered with nothing broken:

2. Think about what was happening in the room at the time. Dropped crockery,
   metal cutlery in a sink, and some television sound effects can set one off.
3. If it happens repeatedly in the same circumstances, the sensor is too
   sensitive or too close to the source of the noise.

If it is showing offline:

4. Take the cover off and remove the battery for ten seconds, then replace it.
5. Wait about thirty seconds for it to reconnect.
6. Check the panel to see whether it has come back.
