---
title: Control panel will not connect to wifi
device_type: control_panel
symptoms: panel offline, no connection, panel says no network, cannot arm remotely, app cannot reach panel
---

The panel loses its connection when the home network changes, when the router
restarts, or when the wifi password has been updated.

1. Check whether other things in the house are online. Try a phone or a laptop.
2. If nothing else is online either, the problem is the internet connection, not the panel.
3. Restart the router. Unplug it, wait thirty seconds, plug it back in, and wait about two minutes.

If the rest of the house is online but the panel is not:

4. On the panel, go to Settings, then Network.
5. Tell me whether your network name appears in the list.
6. Select it and enter the wifi password. This is the password for your home network, not your alarm code.
7. Wait about a minute for it to connect.

If the panel cannot see the network at all, it may be too far from the router.
A panel more than about fifty feet away, or with several walls in between, will
struggle to hold a connection.
